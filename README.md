# Gospel Presentation

A clear, warm presentation of the good news of Jesus Christ — who He is and how to receive eternal life.

## What's here

A single self-contained page (`index.html`) that walks through:

1. **Who Jesus is** — the Son of God, fully God and fully man, the Word made flesh (John 1:1, John 1:14, John 14:6)
2. **Why we need Him** — all have sinned and fall short (Romans 3:23); the wages of sin is death (Romans 6:23)
3. **What He did for us** — God so loved the world that He gave His Son (John 3:16); Christ died for our sins and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4)
4. **How to receive eternal life** — believe in the Lord Jesus (Acts 16:31, John 5:24); by grace through faith, not works (Ephesians 2:8–9)
5. **Assurance** — that you may know that you have eternal life (1 John 5:13)

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain). The page ends with a gentle sample prayer for anyone who wants to receive Christ.

## Viewing

Open `index.html` in any browser, or serve this folder with any static server. The page is mobile-friendly with light and dark themes.
